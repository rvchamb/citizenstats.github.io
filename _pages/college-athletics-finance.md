## The opportunity

College athletics is frequently discussed through rankings, conference changes, media contracts, and headline revenue. A more useful analytical view connects those outcomes to institutional context, spending, subsidies, sport mix, and competitive structure.

## Proposed questions

- How dependent are athletic departments on institutional support?
- How do revenue and spending patterns differ across conferences and institution types?
- Which comparisons remain meaningful after accounting for scale?
- How have major structural changes altered the financial picture?

## Planned approach

The project will follow the same CitizenStats standards used for Chapter 70: documented public sources, explicit grain and definitions, dbt transformations, automated validation, a canonical Neon/Postgres model, governed metrics, and multiple BI consumers.

Source selection and scope are not yet final. This page will become the durable technical record as the project begins.
